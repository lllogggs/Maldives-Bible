# OG resort photo generation prompts

The interface layer is assembled deterministically in `scripts/generate-og-image.py`. Built-in image generation was used only for the two original resort photographs.

## Boardwalk photograph

```text
Create an original, photorealistic luxury travel magazine photograph designed to crop cleanly to a 1200×630 Open Graph card (wide 1.91:1). Eye-level perspective at dawn from a simple weathered timber jetty in the Maldives, the jetty beginning just outside the lower edge and leading diagonally—not perfectly centered—toward one refined open-air pavilion and a small cluster of distant overwater villas. Clear shallow lagoon shifts naturally from pale aqua to deep teal, with visible sand ripples and gentle reflections; soft pearl-blue sky with a trace of warm sunrise, calm and quiet. Architecture should feel authentic, restrained and expensive, not fantastical. Editorial photography for a design-conscious travel journal, understated filmic colors, subtle grain, realistic light and scale, ample sky and water, one memorable focal point. No people, no staged champagne, no boats, no text, no lettering, no logos, no badges, no graphic overlays, no collage, no symmetry, no oversaturated turquoise, no dramatic HDR, no lens flare, no impossible structures. Keep all important content inside a 5% crop-safe margin.
```

## Aerial resort photograph

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: resort thumbnail inside a premium Open Graph comparison card
Primary request: create an original photorealistic aerial travel photograph of an elegant Maldivian private island resort
Scene/backdrop: shallow luminous turquoise lagoon with visible sand and reef texture, a small organic white-sand island with palms, and a restrained row of overwater villas
Style/medium: high-end editorial resort photography, natural and believable rather than glossy CGI
Composition/framing: wide landscape; island and villas fill the frame clearly at small thumbnail size; asymmetrical diagonal composition; no large empty sky
Lighting/mood: bright soft morning light, calm, refined, inviting
Color palette: natural turquoise, deep teal, warm sand, muted palm green
Constraints: photo only; no people, no boats, no text, no logo, no badge, no border, no collage, no heart-shaped island, no perfect circle, no oversaturation, no HDR, no impossible architecture; preserve a crop-safe center area
```
